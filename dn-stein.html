DN Stein: Digital Transformation Project

A university project focused on developing a digital transformation concept for DN Stein, a Sarajevo-based company specializing in natural stone processing, including marble and granite.

  • Course project
  • Analysis and Design of Information Systems
  • Team project
  • Business analysis, digital strategy & website prototype

About the project

The project focused on analyzing DN Stein’s existing business processes and identifying opportunities for digital transformation. The company relied on a combination of phone communication, an existing RMK system and Excel spreadsheets, which created challenges in inventory management, order tracking and customer communication.

Our team developed a digital transformation concept aimed at bringing these processes together into a more integrated and efficient information system.

The proposed solution included a new company website, integration with the existing RMK inventory system, centralized customer and order management, automated communication, and a stronger social media presence.

The project also involved strategic analysis, customer journey mapping, user personas, system requirements and cost considerations. A website prototype was developed to demonstrate how the company’s digital presence could look and function.

My role

As part of a four-member team, I contributed to the research, analysis and development of the proposed digital transformation concept.

My contributions included:

  • Analyzing the company’s existing business processes and identifying operational challenges.
  • Contributing to strategic analyses, including SWOT, PESTEL and VRIO.
  • Participating in the development of customer personas and customer journey mapping to better understand user needs and expectations.
  • Defining business, functional and non-functional system requirements.
  • Contributing to the proposed digital solution, including website development concepts, inventory integration and customer communication improvements.
  • Participating in the creation of the website prototype, helping translate the proposed solution into a visual representation.

Key outcome

The final deliverable was a comprehensive digital transformation proposal supported by a presentation and an interactive website prototype. The project demonstrated how digital tools could improve operational efficiency, data accuracy, customer experience and the company’s overall market visibility.
