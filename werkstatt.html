Werkstatt Studio: Digital Transformation & E-Commerce Strategy

A university project focused on evaluating the digital maturity of Werkstatt Studio, a Sarajevo-based artisan jewelry brand, and identifying opportunities for further digital growth.

  • Course project
  • Digital Transformation and eBusiness
  • Team project
  • Digital maturity assessment, e-commerce analysis & strategic recommendations

About the project

The project explored the digital maturity and transformation journey of Werkstatt Studio, a local jewelry brand established in 2015, known for combining contemporary design with traditional jewelry-making and graphic techniques.

Through a semi-structured interview with the company’s management and an analysis of its existing digital practices, we examined its e-commerce operations, online presence, digital infrastructure, marketing activities and customer experience.

Our analysis covered the company’s transition to Shopify, the integration of Japet software for production and inventory management, the use of social media and digital advertising, and the relationship between online and physical sales channels.

Based on the findings, we identified key challenges and developed a set of short-, medium- and long-term recommendations to support the company’s continued digital transformation.

These included improving inventory management through barcode scanning, introducing customer reviews and automated abandoned-cart emails, developing newsletter marketing, implementing AI-powered customer support, and exploring advanced solutions such as 3D product visualization, virtual fitting and CRM integration.

My role

As part of a five-member team, I contributed to researching, analyzing and evaluating the company’s digital business environment and developing recommendations for its future growth.

My contributions included:

  • Participating in the analysis of Werkstatt Studio’s digital maturity and existing e-commerce practices.
  • Contributing to the SWOT and PESTEL analyses to identify internal capabilities, external challenges and growth opportunities.
  • Examining the company’s digital sales channels, online customer experience and integration of online and offline operations.
  • Identifying opportunities to improve e-commerce performance, customer engagement and digital marketing.
  • Contributing to the development of short-, medium- and long-term digital transformation recommendations.
  • Exploring potential applications of emerging technologies, including AI chatbots, 3D product visualization and virtual fitting solutions.
  • Participating in the development and presentation of the final digital transformation strategy.

Key outcome

The project resulted in a comprehensive digital maturity assessment and a strategic roadmap for Werkstatt Studio’s future development. It highlighted how better use of digital tools, automation, data analytics and customer-focused e-commerce solutions could strengthen operational efficiency, improve the shopping experience and support sustainable international growth.
