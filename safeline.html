SafeLine Event: Digital Safety and Mental Health Youth

A project by the Digital Transformation Association in BiH in partnership with KosovaLive, bringing together young people from Bosnia and Herzegovina and Kosovo through educational activities, shared experiences and new connections.

  • 27–28 April 2026
  • School of Economics and Business, UNSA
  • 90+ participants

Goal

Raising young people’s awareness of digital safety, mental health and the importance of regional cooperation.

My role

  • Technical support
  • PR and marketing
  • Interview video editing
  • Visual design throughout the project

Programme

Two days of workshops for high school and university students were held on 27–28 April 2026 at the School of Economics and Business in Sarajevo, with interactive lectures, group work, practical exercises and discussions. Topics included safe internet use, personal data protection, cyber threats and the impact of the digital environment on mental health.

Result

More than 90 young people took part, including students from Gimnazija SSST, the Sarajevo Medical High School and several university faculties. Active participation and quality discussions showed strong interest in digital skills and mental health.
