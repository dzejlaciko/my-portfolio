Girls Take Notes

A workshop organized as part of a collaboration between iWoman BiH and the U.S. Embassy, designed as a space for young women to exchange perspectives, share experiences and express their ideas.

  • 12 September 2024
  • Homework Hub, Grbavica, Sarajevo
  • About 20 female students

Format

The workshop combined open discussions with creative, hands-on activities. Participants took part in group discussions, brainstorming sessions and interactive exercises, writing associations, thoughts and ideas on flip-chart paper. This collaborative approach encouraged participation, peer-to-peer exchange and a comfortable space for different viewpoints.

My role

I was responsible for technical assistance and general on-site support throughout the event, helping the workshop run smoothly and supporting organizers and participants.

Beyond the event: iWoman BiH

My work with iWoman BiH went beyond individual events. I contributed to the project’s digital presence by writing and publishing articles for the website, creating Instagram visuals and supporting social media communication. It let me combine content writing, visual communication and digital marketing on a platform that empowers and informs young women.
